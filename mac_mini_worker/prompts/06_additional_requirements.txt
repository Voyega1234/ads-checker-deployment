FIELD GUIDANCE

summary.display_title:

* pass -> short reassuring Thai line
* fail -> short line saying what type of issue was found

summary.display_subtitle:

* mention spell and policy counts briefly

summary.executive_summary:

* 1-2 short sentences
* explain whether the message passed, had spelling issues, policy failures, or both

spell_check:

* include only clear spelling or wording issues
* do not include slang, brand names, stylistic spelling, or uncertain cases
* clear spelling/wording fixes flow into revised_message, not a separate field

policy_context:

* use for reviewer guidance only
* include operational cautions, possible approval/disclosure checks, or context notes
* do not treat policy_context as a fail list

policy_check:

* include only concrete policy failures
* each item must be supported by an applicable RULES_BLOCK rule
* do not include spelling issues, advice, uncertainty, or non-applicable rules

policy_check.flagged_text:

* copy the exact risky wording from INPUT_MESSAGE whenever possible
* if the failure is a missing required-on-ad disclosure, use a short description of the missing item

policy_check.issue_title:

* short Thai title of the policy failure

policy_check.issue_detail:

* explain why the visible ad content violates the applicable rule

policy_check.fix_note:

* give one practical fix
* do not add new claims, evidence, approval, guarantee, or unsupported wording

display_reference:

* build only from source and locator supplied in RULES_BLOCK
* if unavailable, use an empty string

DECISION PRIORITY

Fail only when:

1. there is a clear spelling issue, or
2. there is a concrete policy failure supported by an applicable RULES_BLOCK rule.

Return pass when:

* spelling is uncertain
* policy applicability is uncertain
* the rule is unrelated to the ad category
* the concern is only operational or evidence-related but not required to appear in the ad
* the concern is only general caution

Do not over-escalate.
When unsure, do not create spell_check.items or policy_check items.
Put useful uncertainty notes in spell_check.notes or policy_context.reviewer_notes.
